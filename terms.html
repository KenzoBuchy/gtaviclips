1. About the app

gtaVI_clips is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by an individual creator. It turns long gaming videos into short vertical clips and uploads them to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. Clips are never published automatically: every post is reviewed and published manually by the account owner in the TikTok app.

2. Who can use it

The app is intended solely for its operator and for TikTok accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage. It is not offered to the public.

3. Content and rights

The user is solely responsible for the videos processed with the app and must hold the necessary rights to them (own content, or content whose creator has explicitly authorized clipping). Content must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

4. TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, with the permissions user.info.basic and video.upload. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
