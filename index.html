gtaVI_clips

gtaVI_clips is a personal, non-commercial tool that turns long gaming videos into short vertical clips (9:16, with animated subtitles) and uploads them to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. Every post is reviewed and published manually by the account owner in the TikTok app.

It uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API (user.info.basic, video.upload).
